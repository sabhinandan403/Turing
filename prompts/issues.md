## User Prompts

What patterns appear in inpatient encounters when comparing provider specialty and city, patient state, and payer name and city? For each matching set of records that has at least one encounter, report how much activity there is, how many different patients are involved, the most recent encounter timing, the top recorded patient and provider profile details, the top payer contact details, and repeated system wide maximum reference values from Medicare home health, hospice, hospital, inpatient charge, nursing facility, and county birth summary sources.
